Privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Google data Hermes accesses

Hermes is authorized to access one Google account, aperagent.hermes@gmail.com.

  • Gmail — reads, sends, and modifies email.
  • Google Drive, Docs, and Sheets — accesses and manages files, documents, and spreadsheets.
  • Google Calendar — reads and manages calendar entries.
  • Google Contacts — reads contact information with read-only access.

How Hermes uses Google data

Hermes uses Google data only to carry out tasks requested by its owner, such as reading and sending email, looking up contacts, and managing files, documents, spreadsheets, and calendar entries.

Storage and sharing

Hermes stores OAuth tokens and Google data locally on the owner’s computer. Hermes does not sell this data, share it with third parties, or use it for advertising.

This website has no signups, ads, analytics, cookies, or tracking.

Google API Limited Use

Hermes's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and requesting deletion

To revoke access, open your Google Account, go to Security, open Third-party apps & services, and remove Hermes.

To request deletion of locally stored Google data or OAuth tokens, email aperagent.hermes@gmail.com.
